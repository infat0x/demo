I’m interested in Windows internals, reverse engineering, and application security. This blog is where I share technical notes, lab write-ups, and what I learn along the way.

## What I write about

- **Windows internals** - processes, memory, and how the operating system works.
- **Reverse engineering** - notes on binary analysis, debugging, and the tools I use.
- **Application security** - lessons from authorized bug bounty programs and lab exercises.
